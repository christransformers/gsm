Our People

Andrew Goulopoulos

Director

Profile & accreditation

Andrew has over 40 years in legal practice during which he has acquired considerable expertise in many areas of law.

Andrew has been an Accredited Business Law Specialist since 1992, among the first batch to receive such accreditation. Accreditation involves substantial practice experience and a comprehensive examination process testing technical knowledge, writing and practical advocacy skills, with renewal every three years.

He was appointed as a Notary Public in 1996.

Key practice areas

  • Business law including sale and purchase of businesses and business structures.
  • Company and trust formation and advice.
  • Shareholder, unitholder and partnership agreements and disputes.
  • Business succession planning.
  • Distribution and supply agreements.
  • Franchising.
  • Commercial and retail property law, joint venture development agreements and leasing.
  • Estate planning and complex wills.

Clients & professional service

Among the clients Andrew has represented are owners and developers of neighbourhood shopping and bulky goods centres.

Law Institute involvement

Business Law Committee since its inception in 1988; Business Law Dispute Resolution Committee since its inception in 1992; and Business Law Advisory Committee from 1992 until 1997.

Outside the office

Andrew's passions away from the office, apart from family, have included the Carlton Football Club and horse racing and breeding. He played more than 50 games with Carlton in the VFL Reserves in the 1960s and over 100 games with Werribee in the VFA.

He has also enjoyed considerable success in horse racing, including with Salamander, jointly owned with Terry Shiels, which recorded Group 1 successes and notable photo-finish seconds in the Victoria Derby in 1976 and Melbourne Cup in 1979.
