Director

Practice

  • Corporations law and practice.
  • Commercial litigation.
  • Commercial property and retail leasing.

  • Mediation on the LEADER mediation panel and Small Business Commissioner panel.

Client service

James is extremely dedicated to his work. His commitment to clients is reflected in long-standing relationships built through early and full dialogue about concerns and business objectives.

He is accessible and responsive and is well regarded for professional and practical advice.

Professional involvement

Outside the office

His interest in sport includes soccer, where he was a player and served as Chairman of the Australian National Soccer League Tribunal for over twenty years.
