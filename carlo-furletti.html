Carlo Furletti

Background & qualifications

Carlo joined the firm in November 2004. He has over 30 years' experience in business, corporate, commercial and property law, plus retail and commercial leasing, licensing and gaming law.

He is a graduate of Melbourne University Law School and has been engaged in legal practice as a Principal since 1971. He is a Notary Public and can advise on matters with international law implications.

Key practice areas

  • Property law including real-estate developments, subdivisions, commercial and retail leasing.
  • Business and commercial law, corporations law and practice.
  • Estate planning, wills and testamentary matters.
  • Transfer of Alpine Resort properties and interests.

Public service & policy impact

  • Former member of the Victorian Legislative Council (1996-2002).
  • Member of the Parliamentary Law Reform Committee (1996-1999).
  • Former Shadow Minister for Small Business and Natural Resources, Energy and Ports (2000-2002).
  • Extensive experience interpreting and implementing legislation and developing and drafting legislation and regulations.
  • Contributed to legislation concerning retail leases, liquor licensing, gaming and other areas affecting small and medium-sized businesses.

Professional memberships

Member of the Law Institute of Victoria's Commercial Law Section and Property Law Section and its Business Law Committee.

Recognition

In 2005, Carlo received the Italian Government honorific title of Cavaliere Ufficiale of the Republic of Italy for his contribution to the Italian community.
