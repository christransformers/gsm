Athanasia Balaskas

Associate

Family law practice

Athanasia joined the firm in 2003 and is a member of the Family Law Section of the Law Institute of Victoria.

She practices in all aspects of Family Law and deals with children and property issues arising from the breakdown of domestic relationships.

She advises on protecting assets prior to and during a relationship, including cohabitation and binding financial agreements.

Insolvency, recovery & litigation

Her other areas of practice include insolvency law, debt recovery and contract enforcement, with specific expertise acting for judgment creditors enforcing judgments through winding up and bankruptcy applications.

Her litigation experience extends to all jurisdictions, including the Victorian Civil and Administrative Tribunal.
