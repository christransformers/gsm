Director

Key practice areas

  • Domestic and Commercial Property transactions.
  • Adverse Possession Applications and Conversion of General Law Land to Land under the Transfer of Land Act.

Community involvement

Terry was an active member of the Rotary Club of Collingwood for over 35 years and was presented with a Paul Harris Fellowship for his contribution to Rotary.

For a number of years Terry was also a member of the Council of the Collingwood College of Technical and Further Education, serving on the Legal and Finance Committee.
